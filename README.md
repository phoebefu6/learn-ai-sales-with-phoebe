## AI + Sales

AI made sending free, so replying got expensive. Two tracks, sixteen 45-minute sessions:

- **Leader track (6)** - what AI actually changes in a sales org, the evidence versus the vendor deck, a forecast people believe, enablement without surveillance, the compliance rails, and a rollout that survives comp season.
- **Rep track (10)** - one running deal end to end. Eight named accounts at Cadence, anchor account Northwind Logistics, from a cold row to an inheritable closed record.

A live outreach simulator runs on the key pages: toggle the five levers of a cold email and watch the predicted reply rate climb from 2.8% to 19.2% on one prospect, and from 1.7% to 10.1% across the sequence. The sixth toggle is an anti-lever - more personalisation tokens, fewer replies - and finding out why is the point.

Taught from Gartner's B2B seller and buyer research, published 2025-2026 cold-email benchmark reports, and the outreach compliance regimes (PDPA and DNC, CAN-SPAM, CASL, GDPR). Compliance content is orientation, not legal advice.
