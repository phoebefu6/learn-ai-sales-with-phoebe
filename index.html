Learn AI Sales with Phoebe

Two tracks into selling that still sounds human

AI made sending free, so replying got expensive. Platform-average cold reply rates fell from about 5.1% to about 3.4% in two years while volume went vertical - and over the same period Gartner found that sellers who genuinely partner with AI are 3.7x more likely to hit quota, that AI hands each seller back nearly five hours a week, and that 72% of sales organisations reinvest none of it. The tool is not the intervention. The habit is. A leader track gives VPs and CROs the evidence, the forecast rebuild, the enablement model and the compliance rails. A rep track runs one real deal end to end - eight named accounts at Cadence, anchor account Northwind Logistics - with a live outreach simulator on the key pages where you watch one email climb from 2.8% to 19.2% predicted reply, and find out for yourself why the sixth toggle never helps.
